A note on historical accuracy

This is a working research directory, not a claim that every site in Bihar has been documented. Names may have alternative spellings, and some listings identify a town or shrine complex rather than a single monument. Silsila affiliations, dates, saint biographies, custodians and Urs dates should be verified from reliable historical works, inscriptions and the institutions concerned before publication as definitive facts. A listing does not imply endorsement of any particular religious belief or practice.
