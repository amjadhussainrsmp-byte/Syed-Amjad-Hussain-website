Biography

A life devoted to history, heritage and literature.

Based in Sheikhpura, Bihar, Hussain's work is concerned with documenting historical personalities, religious traditions and lesser-known aspects of the region's past.

Syed Amjad Hussain is an Indian ethnographer, independent researcher, author, and poet, whose work primarily focuses on history, Sufi heritage, Islamic scholarship, regional literature, and the cultural history of Bihar. He is also described as a historian for his research and documentation of historical personalities, religious traditions, and lesser-known aspects of the region's past.

He is particularly recognised for his book Bihar Aur Sufivad, a National Bestseller that explores the history and traditions of Sufism in Bihar.

01 · Early life and family background

An inheritance of learning and service

Hussain's family background provides an important historical and cultural context for his interests in Islamic scholarship, genealogy, religious institutions and the heritage of Bihar.

Syed Amjad Hussain was born to Syed Ahmad Hussain Azizi in a family with a longstanding association with Islamic scholarship, religious education, and community service. His father, Syed Ahmad Hussain Azizi, is an educationist and social worker who founded Madrasatul Banat Azizul Uloom, Jamwara, Sheikhpura, and served as its Headmaster. He also served as the Secretary of Madrasa Jamia Habibia, Jamwara, Sheikhpura. He holds a postgraduate qualification in Labour and Social Welfare from Tilka Manjhi Bhagalpur University, Bhagalpur, Bihar.

His grandfather, Hafiz Dr Syed Aashique Hussain, was a homeopathic physician and a Hafiz of the Holy Qur'an. He received his professional education from The Calcutta Homoeopathic Medical College and Hospital and his religious education from the Madrasa of Khanqah-e-Maulaiya, Maula Nagar, Surajgarha, Lakhisarai, Bihar.

Hussain traces his ancestry to Hazrat Syed Shah Jamaluddin Jajneri, son of Hazrat Syed Ahmed Jajneri Zaidi, whose family lineage is associated with Hazrat Syed Abul Farah Wasti, traditionally connected with Wasit, Iran, before his arrival in the Indian subcontinent.

Family history, religious learning and community institutions form an important part of the cultural setting from which his research interests emerged.

This family background has provided an important historical and cultural context for Hussain's interests in Islamic scholarship, genealogy, religious institutions, and the heritage of Bihar.

02 · Education

From Sheikhpura to higher studies

His educational journey began in Sheikhpura, where his interest in history and social sciences gradually took shape.

Modern Academy, Sheikhpura

He began his schooling at Modern Academy, Sheikhpura, where he studied up to the Upper Kindergarten level.

All Saints Academy, Sheikhpura

He subsequently attended All Saints Academy, completing his studies there up to Standard III.

Delhi Public School, Sheikhpura

He joined Delhi Public School, Sheikhpura, in Class IV, during the year the institution was established in the district. He continued his education there until completing his matriculation (Class X).

Interest in history and social sciences

During his school years, his interest in history and social sciences developed considerably under the influence of his history teacher, Md Afzal Iqbal. The teacher's encouragement, combined with Hussain's existing interest in historical narratives and personalities, helped strengthen his inclination towards historical subjects.

SADN Convent School, Sheikhpura

Following matriculation, he enrolled at SADN Convent School, Sheikhpura, Bihar, where he pursued the Science stream with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) at the intermediate level.

Higher education

For higher education, he moved to West Bengal and graduated from Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT) in 2026. In the same year, he enrolled at Lovely Professional University, Punjab, for postgraduate studies. He also aspires to pursue doctoral research in the future.

03 · Research interests and scholarly activities

Documenting Bihar's historical and Sufi heritage

His research brings together historical references, literary materials, local traditions and regional accounts.

Hussain's research interests encompass the history of Bihar, Sufism, Islamic intellectual traditions, regional heritage, genealogy, and the documentation of historical personalities and institutions.

His work particularly concerns the historical development of Sufi traditions in Bihar, the biographies of saints and scholars, khanqahs and dargahs, Islamic educational institutions, and the preservation of lesser-known historical narratives.

He is interested in bringing together historical references, literary materials, local traditions, and regional accounts to make underdocumented aspects of Bihar's heritage more accessible to readers and researchers.

He is associated with Lost Muslim Heritage of Bihar, a heritage documentation initiative operating through a Facebook page under HeritageTimes.in, founded by Md Umar Ashraf. The initiative focuses on documenting and bringing attention to the historical and cultural legacy of Muslim communities in Bihar.

As an independent researcher, Hussain continues to explore subjects relating to regional history, Sufi heritage, Islamic scholarship, and the literary contributions of personalities associated with Bihar.

04 · Books and literary works

Three major book-length historical projects

The projects reflect his interest in Bihar's historical personalities, regional Urdu literary history and the development of Sufi traditions.

01

The Eternals of Bihar

The Eternals of Bihar is a historical literary project focusing on approximately twenty-five Muslim leaders, scholars, and distinguished personalities associated with Bihar. The work reflects Hussain's interest in documenting the contributions and historical significance of individuals who have shaped the region's intellectual, religious, and social landscape.

02

Akhtar Orenvi: Bihar Mein Urdu Sahitya Ke Nirmata

This work explores the life and literary background of Akhtar Orenvi, a personality associated with Urain (Oren), Lakhisarai, Bihar. The subject represents Hussain's interest in regional Urdu literary history and the documentation of literary personalities from Bihar. The manuscript was subsequently withdrawn from publication by the author for personal religious reasons.

03

Bihar Aur Sufivad

Published by Rajmangal Prakashan on 27 April 2025, Bihar Aur Sufivad is Hussain's most widely recognised work. The 308-page book examines the historical development, personalities, traditions, and cultural influence of Sufism in Bihar.

Explore the book →

Inside Bihar Aur Sufivad

Organised into seven sections, the publication covers subjects including Islam and Sufism, Sufi silsilas, biographical accounts of twenty Sufi personalities, and associated literary material, including manqabats. It draws upon Persian and Urdu sources, historical references, visits to khanqahs and dargahs, and more than 200 sources.

The foreword was written by Dr Muhammad Sajjad Alam Rizvi of Presidency University, Kolkata. Professor Dr Uzma Azhar Ali of Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi, also provided suggestions during the manuscript's preparation.

The book achieved National Bestseller status shortly after becoming available for sale on 1 May 2025. It secured the fourth position in Amazon's Indian Writings Bestseller category on its first evening of availability and reached the first position on 4 May 2025. Its sales and bestseller recognition were subsequently covered by Dainik Bhaskar and Dainik Jagran.

The book was formally launched on 29 June 2025 at the Bihar Industries Association Auditorium, Patna, under the banner of Zenith Social Foundation. The event was attended by several prominent personalities, including IPS Vikas Vaibhav, Maulana Ghulam Rasool Balyawi, Shakeel Hashmi, and Md Asharul Haque, alongside journalists, writers, researchers, and other distinguished guests.

The book received appreciation from several personalities associated with academia, religious scholarship, public administration, and social development, including Maulana Ghulam Rasool Balyawi, Muhammad Mudassir Ashrafi, Professor Sunil Jaglan, and IAS officer Dr Muhammad Shahid Raza Khan.

06 · Literary interests

Urdu poetry and the literary tradition

Alongside historical research and non-fiction writing, poetry remains an important part of his literary interests.

In addition to historical research and non-fiction writing, Syed Amjad Hussain has an interest in Urdu poetry. He writes under the poetic pen name Amjad (امجدؔ).

His literary interests extend to traditional Urdu poetic expression, spirituality, devotional literature, and the cultural and intellectual traditions of the Urdu-speaking world.

07 · Academic aspirations and vision

Preserving heritage and widening access to history

Hussain intends to continue his academic development alongside his independent research activities, with the long-term aspiration of pursuing doctoral research.

His broader objective is to contribute to the documentation and preservation of Bihar's historical, religious, and literary heritage, particularly subjects that remain insufficiently represented in mainstream historical literature.

Through his books, research articles, and literary contributions, he seeks to encourage greater engagement with regional history and make historical knowledge accessible to wider audiences, particularly younger generations.

Syed Amjad Hussain continues to work as an independent researcher, ethnographer, author, and poet, with a particular focus on Bihar's history, Sufi traditions, Islamic scholarship, and regional cultural heritage.
