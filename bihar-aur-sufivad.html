About the book

Recovering the stories behind a living tradition.

Bihar Aur Sufivad is a study of the people, places, lineages, and texts that shaped the region’s Sufi heritage.

Drawing on Persian and Urdu material, published references, and visits to khanqahs and dargahs, the book brings together historical and literary perspectives on Sufism in Bihar.

It explores the connections between spiritual traditions and regional history, with attention to the lives and legacies of Sufi figures associated with Bihar. Its aim is to make this heritage more accessible to readers while encouraging deeper engagement with historical sources.

“A journey through the spiritual and literary memory of Bihar.”

Inside the book

History, lineages and literary memory.

The book approaches Bihar’s Sufi heritage through interconnected themes rather than treating it as a story of isolated sites or personalities.

01

Islam and Sufism

An introduction to Sufi thought and its place in the broader religious and intellectual history.

02

Sufi Silsilas

Spiritual lineages and networks that connect teachers, disciples, khanqahs, and regions.

03

Lives of the Sufis

Biographical sketches of Sufi figures associated with Bihar and their historical legacies.

04

Khanqahs and Dargahs

Places of devotion, learning, hospitality, and collective memory across the region.

05

Persian and Urdu Sources

Literary and historical materials that help preserve regional narratives and traditions.

06

Manqabat and Devotional Verse

The poetic expressions through which reverence and spiritual memory are carried forward.
