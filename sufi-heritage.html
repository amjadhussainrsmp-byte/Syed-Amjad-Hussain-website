Let's Discover Bihar's Sufi Past

✦

Bihar occupies an important place in the history of Sufism in the Indian subcontinent. Its spiritual heritage is associated with Sufi scholars, mystics, khanqahs, dargahs, literary traditions and networks of learning that developed across different regions over several centuries.

This section brings together historical information about Bihar's Sufi heritage, with particular attention to documentary sources, local traditions, Persian and Urdu writings, and the preservation of regional history.

Research Principles

History Through Sources

The purpose of this project is to document Bihar's Sufi heritage through historical research and responsible presentation of evidence. Biographical traditions, shrine histories and local accounts deserve careful study alongside documentary records.

Research entries may draw upon:

  • Persian and Urdu historical works and biographical dictionaries.
  • Manuscripts, inscriptions and archival documents.
  • Published academic studies and regional histories.
  • Records preserved by khanqahs, shrines and local institutions.
  • Oral histories, clearly identified as oral testimony.

Dates, genealogies, spiritual affiliations and historical claims should be included with references wherever possible. Unverified traditions should be identified as such rather than presented as established historical facts.
