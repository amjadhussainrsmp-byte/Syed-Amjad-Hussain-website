Historical Profile

Hazrat Makhdoom Shaykh Shah Abdul Aziz Maneri

Hazrat Makhdoom Shaykh Shah Abdul Aziz Maneri was a Sufi saint associated with the historic spiritual heritage of Maner Sharif, Bihar. He was the youngest son of Hazrat Imam Muhammad Taj Faqih Hashmi Zubairi and the younger brother of Hazrat Shaykh Shah Ismail Maneri and Hazrat Shaykh Shah Israil Maneri. His family lineage connects Maner Sharif with the wider Sufi heritage of Bihar.

Biography

Hazrat Makhdoom Shaykh Shah Abdul Aziz Maneri occupies a notable place in the spiritual and genealogical history of Bihar. Associated with the illustrious tradition of Maner Sharif, he belonged to a family whose journey from Jerusalem to the Indian subcontinent became an important chapter in the history of the region's Islamic heritage. He was the youngest son of Hazrat Imam Muhammad Taj Faqih Hashmi Zubairi, who, according to the historical tradition preserved about the family, arrived at Maner Sharif, Bihar, in 1180, accompanied by his children, grandchildren, and followers. This migration established a lasting connection between the family and the spiritual landscape of Bihar, a region that would become home to numerous distinguished Sufi personalities and centres of religious learning. Hazrat Shah Abdul Aziz Maneri was the youngest brother of Hazrat Shaykh Shah Ismail Maneri and Hazrat Shaykh Shah Israil Maneri. His place within this distinguished family makes him an important figure in understanding the familial connections that shaped the history of the Maner Sharif tradition. His legacy continued through his sons, Hazrat Shaykh Shah Sulaiman Langar Zameen and Hazrat Shaykh Shah Jalaal Maneri, both of whom are associated with significant spiritual centres in Bihar. Hazrat Shaykh Shah Sulaiman Langar Zameen is buried at Kako Sharif, in present-day Jehanabad district, while Hazrat Shaykh Shah Jalaal Maneri rests at Maner Sharif. Their resting places preserve a tangible connection between the family's history and two important locations in Bihar's religious landscape. The story of Hazrat Makhdoom Shaykh Shah Abdul Aziz Maneri is, therefore, inseparable from the broader history of the family to which he belonged. Through his ancestry and descendants, his name forms part of a historical narrative that connects Jerusalem with Maner Sharif and extends towards other centres of spiritual significance in Bihar. For researchers exploring the genealogy of Bihar's Sufi families, the life of Hazrat Shah Abdul Aziz Maneri offers a valuable point of reference. His familial relationships illuminate the interconnected heritage of the Maner Sharif tradition and provide a foundation for further study of the personalities, lineages, and sacred sites that have shaped Bihar's rich Sufi history..

Chronology & Historical Context

Hazrat Makhdoom Shaykh Shah Abdul Aziz Maneri belonged to the distinguished family of Hazrat Imam Muhammad Taj Faqih Hashmi Zubairi, who, according to family tradition, migrated from Jerusalem to Maner Sharif, Bihar, around 1180 CE. As the youngest son of Imam Taj Faqih, Shah Abdul Aziz formed part of the early family lineage associated with Maner Sharif's spiritual heritage. His sons, Hazrat Shaykh Shah Sulaiman Langar Zameen and Hazrat Shaykh Shah Jalaal Maneri, are associated with the historic Sufi centres of Kako Sharif and Maner Sharif, respectively.
