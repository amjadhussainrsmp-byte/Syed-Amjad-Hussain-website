A national milestone

Invitation to the 76th Republic Day celebrations

In January 2025, Syed Amjad Hussain was invited by the Ministry of Defence, Government of India, to attend the 76th Republic Day celebrations in New Delhi. For a young author and researcher from Bihar, the invitation was a memorable milestone and an encouragement to continue contributing to literature, historical inquiry and public life.

NEW DELHI · JANUARY 2025
Invitations & milestones

Recognition beyond the page

Literature · Research

Bihar Aur Sufivad

The book documents Bihar’s rich Sufi heritage, bringing together historical personalities, traditions and cultural memory. It has helped introduce a wider readership to stories and histories that have often remained rooted in local communities.

Madinah · International invitation

Invitation to Madinah Book Fair

Syed Amjad Hussain received an invitation from the Literature, Publishing and Translation Commission of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to participate in the Madinah Book Fair. The invitation represented an opportunity to share his literary interests and Bihar’s Sufi and cultural heritage with an international audience. It is recorded here as an invitation, not as confirmation of attendance.

Ongoing · Public engagement

Research, writing and service

Beyond formal honours, Amjad continues to write, research and document Bihar’s cultural heritage while remaining involved in community-oriented work. These efforts remain at the heart of his public and literary journey.

A note on recognition

Honours are encouragement, not the end goal.

For Amjad, each recognition is a reminder to keep reading, researching, writing and serving. The purpose of the work remains the preservation of overlooked histories and the sharing of knowledge with future generations.

Accuracy & documentation

Keeping the record precise.

Awards and invitations can have different official titles and dates. For formal publication, the wording on the original certificate or invitation should take precedence. The page can be updated as additional verified details become available.
