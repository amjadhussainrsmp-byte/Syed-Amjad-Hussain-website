The publication archive

A growing record of research, literature and regional history.

Writing is a way of preserving knowledge and making it accessible beyond the moment in which it is discovered. This page brings together published books and other writings associated with Syed Amjad Hussain's literary and historical interests.

Particular attention is given to Bihar's Sufi heritage, historical personalities, Persian and Urdu sources, literary culture and regional memory.

Each publication has its own purpose. Together, they reflect an ongoing commitment to reading carefully, documenting responsibly and sharing knowledge with a wider audience.

What the writing explores

01

Sufism & spiritual history

Sufi traditions, spiritual lineages, khanqahs, dargahs and the historical development of Sufism in Bihar.

02

Bihar's regional heritage

Historical personalities, places, local traditions and cultural memories that form part of Bihar's past.

03

Persian & Urdu sources

Historical writings, biographical literature and textual sources that can help illuminate people and institutions.

04

Urdu literature

Literary figures, writing traditions and the contribution of Urdu authors to the cultural history of Bihar.

05

Historical biographies

Documenting the lives and contributions of scholars, writers, Sufi figures and other historical personalities.

Research & documentation

Examining sources, comparing accounts and presenting historical material with context and appropriate care.
