About the Book

A Book About Bihar, Told Through Its People

The Eternals of Bihar is the first published book by Syed Amjad Hussain and an early reflection of his enduring fascination with Bihar, its people and its history. Published by Notion Press in January 2023, the book emerged from the perspective of a young writer seeking to draw attention to individuals whose lives and contributions form part of Bihar's story.

For Amjad, Bihar has never been merely a geographical region. It is a land shaped by centuries of scholarship, literature, spirituality, politics, social movements and cultural exchange. Its history has been enriched by people from different backgrounds whose influence can outlive their own times.

The book grows out of a simple but enduring idea: to understand a place, one must look beyond its borders and events to the people who helped shape its identity.

The Eternals of Bihar is an attempt to remember them.

People may pass away, but the meaning of their lives can continue through the generations that remember them.

The central idea behind The Eternals of Bihar: preserving the memory of people through the written word.

The Ideas Behind the Title

Why “The Eternals”?

The title expresses the belief that a meaningful life can continue to influence society long after its owner is gone. People endure through the knowledge, ideas and contributions they leave behind.

History Is Human

Behind Every Legacy Is a Life.

Bihar Through Its People

Looking Beyond Familiar Narratives

Bihar's history is often told through major political developments and widely known historical figures. Yet the story of the region is far larger than any single event or personality.

Its cultural identity has also been shaped by writers, poets, scholars, social workers, thinkers, educators and countless other individuals whose work forms part of the society around them.

Looking at history through people brings its human dimension closer to the reader. It encourages us to consider the lives, circumstances, ideas and efforts behind the events we remember.

Behind every achievement is a person. Behind every institution is a story. Behind every historical period are lives that deserve to be understood.
A Writer's Responsibility

Preserving Memory for the Next Generation

Historical memory can fade when names become unfamiliar, stories are no longer retold and contributions disappear from public awareness. Writing is one way to keep the conversation alive.

The Purpose

To Remember

For Amjad, writing about historical personalities is connected to the responsibility of preserving knowledge. Recording a life or contribution can help make it accessible to readers who might otherwise never encounter that story.

The Possibility

To Pass Knowledge Forward

A book is a bridge between generations. It allows readers to revisit the past, learn from the experiences of others and develop a deeper understanding of the society in which they live.

A Book, A Beginning

Some Lives End. Their Stories Need Not.

The Eternals of Bihar remains an important part of Syed Amjad Hussain's literary journey because it captures the beginning of his relationship with historical writing. It grew from curiosity, an interest in Bihar and a belief in the enduring significance of human contribution.

The book is a reminder that the past is not preserved by dates and events alone. It is also preserved by remembering the people whose lives gave those events meaning.

Their work can remain. Their ideas can remain. Their stories can remain—and through books, their memory can endure.
