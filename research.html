Exploring the past.

Research Profile

History deserves to be examined, documented and remembered.

Syed Amjad Hussain is an independent researcher, author and historian whose work focuses particularly on Sufism and the historical heritage of Bihar.

His research explores the lives of Sufi saints, the development of Sufi orders, the history of khanqahs and dargahs, and the contribution of religious and literary traditions to the region's cultural landscape.

A central concern of this work is the documentation of historical personalities, places and traditions that may receive limited attention in broader historical narratives. The aim is to bring together available evidence and make regional history more accessible to readers and future researchers.

This is an independent scholarly undertaking, not a university- affiliated research programme.

Areas of Inquiry

Fields of research

Research interests connecting regional history, Sufi traditions, textual sources and the preservation of cultural memory.

01

Sufism in Bihar

Historical study of Sufi traditions in Bihar, their intellectual and spiritual lineages, the development of khanqahs, and their place in the region's social and cultural history.

02

Sufi Orders & Biographical History

Documentation of Sufi silsilas, scholarly relationships, biographies of saints, and the historical contexts in which spiritual and intellectual traditions developed.

03

Sheikhpura & Regional Heritage

Research into the historical personalities, sacred sites, local traditions and cultural memory associated with Sheikhpura and its surrounding areas in Bihar.

04

Persian & Urdu Historical Sources

Engagement with Persian and Urdu writings, historical biographies, manuscripts and published works to investigate historical claims and recover information about people, places and institutions.

05

Islamic Intellectual & Literary History

Study of Islamic scholarship, literary traditions and the historical contributions of scholars, writers and spiritual figures, with particular attention to their regional contexts.

06

Heritage Documentation & Preservation

Recording historical information about dargahs, khanqahs, monuments and lesser-documented sites, and encouraging responsible preservation of regional heritage.

Research Approach

Evidence before assertion.

Historical research requires careful reading, comparison of sources and attention to the circumstances in which accounts were produced. The objective is to distinguish documented information from later tradition and interpretation.

  1. 01

    Textual Study

    Examining available historical books, biographical literature and Persian and Urdu material relevant to the subject under investigation.

    2. 02

    Source Comparison

    Comparing accounts across available sources, noting agreements, differences, dates and variations in names or historical details.

  2. 03

    Field & Heritage Observation

    Visiting relevant historical sites where possible and documenting their present condition, local associations and available historical information.

  3. 04

    Responsible Documentation

    Presenting findings with appropriate context and acknowledging uncertainty where the surviving evidence does not support a definitive conclusion.
