ABOUT THE AUTHOR

A life shaped by
history and words.

Syed Amjad Hussain is an ethnographer, author, poet, historian, independent research scholar, and social worker from Sheikhpura, Bihar. His interests centre on Sufism, Islam, regional history, and the preservation of literary and cultural heritage.

Through books, research, and literary writing, he seeks to bring overlooked histories and regional voices into wider view—while keeping sources, context, and careful documentation at the heart of the work.
